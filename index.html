05 / HOW WE WORK

Know the steps before we build.

We begin with the requirement, not the code. Scope, price, schedule and responsibilities should be agreed before implementation.

01

Discuss the goal

Share the users, business problem, current workflow and desired outcome.

02

Agree the scope

Review priorities, deliverables, responsibilities, timeline and budget options.

03

Build and review

Implement agreed milestones, test important flows and gather feedback.

04

Hand over and support

Agree deployment, access, documentation and any ongoing support arrangements.

BEFORE WE START

Questions worth asking before you spend.

Clear expectations make a better project. Here are the first things we would discuss.

Ask about your own project ↗
How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope, integrations and support required. Share your requirements and budget range so we can discuss options. We do not publish a one-size-fits-all quote.

Can you improve a website or application we already own?

Yes, improvement and maintenance work can be discussed. We would first review the current system, access arrangements and technical risks before proposing changes.

How long will development take?

Timelines depend on complexity, approvals and dependencies. A delivery schedule and milestones should be discussed after requirements are understood.

Who owns the finished software and source code?

Ownership, licensing, hosting, access and handover terms should be documented in the project agreement. Do not assume every engagement uses the same terms.

Do you work with clients outside your city?

We are based in Nigeria and welcome enquiries from organisations in other locations. The practical delivery and support arrangements are agreed for each engagement.
