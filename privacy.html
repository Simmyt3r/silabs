Privacy information

Effective October 2026. This page explains how the current Silabs informational website handles enquiries.

Information you choose to share

Our project enquiry form prepares a message for WhatsApp or a draft for your email application. The website code does not submit or store the details on a Silabs server. You must choose Send inside WhatsApp or your email application to complete the enquiry. WhatsApp or your email provider may then process the information under its own policies.

Communication

If you email or contact us through WhatsApp, the information you provide is handled through those services and used to respond to your enquiry or discuss the work you request. Avoid sending passwords, bank-card details or highly sensitive information through a general enquiry.

External services

This site requests typefaces from Google Fonts. Links to LinkedIn, WhatsApp and product websites take you to independent services with their own privacy practices. Standard hosting infrastructure may create basic server or security logs outside this site's code.

Tracking

The website code does not include advertising pixels, behavioural analytics scripts or a cookie-based user tracking system.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to silabtechnologies@gmail.com.
